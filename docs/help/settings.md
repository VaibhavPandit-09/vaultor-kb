# Settings and appearance

Open **Settings** from the sidebar, application menu or palette. Search settings or browse Appearance, Workspace, Layout & previews and Keyboard. Values show their scope; device preferences and workspace preferences differ. Watch Saved / Saving / Not saved; Retry is available for failed persistence.

## Appearance and motion

Choose theme/accent and available transparency/appearance controls. Vaultor shares its design tokens across screens. Theme transitions respect reduced motion and keep geometry stable. Motion preferences tune structural transitions; correctness never requires waiting for an animation. Android has its own reader appearance controls rather than all desktop settings.

## Workspace and layout

Use the actual labeled controls for saving behavior, pane limits/opening policy, sidebar and previews. **Maximum visible panes** controls viewing slots, not the total Open Notes sessions. Sidebar category/quick-access preferences remain device/session scoped and are not portable workspace content. [Navigation](navigation.md).

## Keyboard

Select a binding to record a replacement for the current platform. Escape cancels recording. Reserved browser/editor/history conflicts are refused. Reset can restore a binding or an explicitly reviewed group of settings. Keyboard Help uses the resolved bindings, including your changes, rather than a second fixed list.

Same-field settings changed on multiple clients are last committed wins; this is not collaborative typing. [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
